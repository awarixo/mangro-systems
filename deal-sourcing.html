The Problem

Most of Europe's deal flow never reaches an English-only buyer.

01

Language

Outside the UK and Ireland, most SME listings are published only in the local language — German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, Scandinavian. If you only read English, you're missing most of the continent's deal flow entirely, not just finding it inconvenient.

02

Duplication

Brokers cross-post the same deal across multiple sites under different titles and rounded figures — sometimes the "separate" national sites are even the same parent company. Setting up alerts yourself means duplicate noise, not a clean feed.

03

Noise

Native saved-search alerts are keyword and category matches, not real mandate matching — they can't track sector, size, and geography together. What comes back is mostly irrelevant.
