The problem

Europe's small-business deal flow is scattered across dozens of sites and languages.

01

Too many places to look

National portals, regional brokers, chamber-of-commerce succession exchanges. Each has its own format, its own account, its own alerts.

02

Most of it isn't in English

Outside the UK and Ireland, listings are written in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Swedish and more. If you only read English, you miss most of the market.

03

Alerts match words, not deals

A saved search returns every listing with the keyword in it. It can't tell a business that clears your EBITDA floor from one that never states a figure.

How it works

You set the criteria. We do the watching.

Step 1

Tell us what you're buying

Country or region, sector, size (turnover or EBITDA), and anything you rule out. More than one mandate is fine.

Step 2

We read every listing

Each listing's own page is read and its figures extracted: turnover, EBITDA, employees, reason for sale. Matching runs on those figures, not on keywords.

Step 3

Twice a week, up to five

Every Tuesday and Friday you get up to five new matches, numbered, every link opened that day to confirm it is live. You never see the same listing twice.

Questions

Before you ask.

Where do the listings come from?

Public business-for-sale sites: national marketplaces, regional and specialist brokers, and succession exchanges, across the UK, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Spain, Portugal, Italy, the Benelux, the Nordics and Poland. They are on-market listings, not off-market deals. What we add is watching all of them, every day, and filtering them to you.

Can't I just set up alerts myself?

On the sites you know, in the languages you read, yes. The hard part is the other forty-odd, in languages you don't read, where the figures are written six different ways. That is the part we take off your desk.

Why only five at a time?

Because five checked listings get read, and a list of fifty doesn't. Behind every send we hold the other matches, ranked, and tell you how many there are, so you can ask for more in your area.

What happens after the free week?

The free week is two sends. If they are useful, it continues as a monthly subscription. If not, it stops; there is nothing to cancel.
